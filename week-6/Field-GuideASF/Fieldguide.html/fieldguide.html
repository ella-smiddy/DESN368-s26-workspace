LEARNING THE CARDS

  • 1: Move a pawn forward 1 space OR move one out of Start.
  • 2: Move a pawn forward 2 spaces OR move one out of Start.
  • 3: Move forward 3 spaces.
  • 4: Move backward 4 spaces.
  • 5: Move forward 5 spaces.
  • 7: Move forward 7 spaces.
  • 8: Move forward 8 spaces.
  • 10: Move forward 10 spaces OR move backward 1 space.
  • 11: Move forward 11 spaces OR switch places with an opponent's pawn on the main track.
  • 12: Move forward 12 spaces.
  • Sorry!: Take a pawn from your Start and swap it with any opponent's pawn on the board, bumping them back to start. If you have no pawns in "Start" move backward 4 spaces instead.

GAME SETUP

Each player chooses a color and puts their 4 pawns in their matching Start circle. Place the card deck face down.

Going First: The youngest player goes first, and turns move clockwise.

Drawing Cards: On your turn, draw the top card, follow its instructions, and place it in a discard pile.

Getting Out of Start: You can only move a pawn out of the Start area if you draw a 1, a 2, or a "Sorry!" card.

OBJECTIVE

The goal of the game is simple.

Move all four pawns from Start to Home by drawing cards. The first player to do so, wins!

GAMEPLAY

Goal:
Move all 4 of your pawns from Start to Home before everyone else.

Setup

  • Each player picks a color.
  • Put all 4 pawns in your Start area.
  • Shuffle the cards.

Taking Turns

  1. Draw one card.
  2. Follow the card’s instructions.
  3. Move one of your pawns.

Basic Rules

  1. You need a 1 or 2 card to leave Start.
  2. If you land on another player’s pawn, they go back to Start.
  3. Pawns move around the board toward their Home space.

Special Cards

  • Sorry! Card: Replace one of your opponent’s pawns with one from your Start.
  • Slide Spaces: If you land on the start of another color’s slide, slide to the end and bump any pawns off the slide back to Start.

Winning

The first player to get all 4 pawns Home wins!
